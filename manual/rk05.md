# RK05 — removable cartridge disk drive

[← Manual index](README.md) · device names: **`rk0` … `rk7`** · source: `10.02_devices/2_src/rk05.cpp`

## What it represents

The RK05 is the removable 14" cartridge disk drive that hangs off an [RK11/RKV11
controller](rk11.md). The emulation models the drive's seek behaviour, its sector counter and its
status lines (ready, write protect, seek incomplete, drive unsafe) as the controller sees them
over the drive cable.

| Cylinders | Heads | Sectors | Sector size | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 203 | 2 | 12 | 512 bytes | 2,494,464 bytes |

Eight units are created by the [RK11](rk11.md) as `rk0` … `rk7`.

## Parameters

The RK05 adds no parameters of its own. It has the [common device
parameters](common-parameters.md) and the [storage drive parameters](storage-drives.md) — in
practice `image` is the only one you set, plus `emulation_speed` if you want seeks to go faster
than the real 10 ms/step.

Unlike the [RL01/RL02](rl0102.md), the RK05 emulation has no separate power switch or RUN/STOP
button: enabling the drive with a mounted image is enough.

## Typical use

```
en rk
en rk0
sd rk0
p image ../diskimages/v6bin.rk05.dsk
```
